## HVAC system type (inferred from permits only)

Gas forced-air furnaces (4) with split and packaged DX cooling, rooftop condensing units (6 per the 2013 electrical permit), exposed spiral ductwork. All replaced in kind in 2013, so the current plant is about 13 years old. Gas water heaters for bathrooms and kitchen. Benchmark gas use of 2,000-3,400 therms/yr is consistent with furnaces plus water heating. No broker material describing HVAC was accessible.
